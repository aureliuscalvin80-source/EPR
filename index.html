Recognised headers: SKU / Kode, Name / Nama, Unit / Satuan (PCS or SET), Qty / Stok, Price / Harga, Tax / PPN, Min. Rows with the same SKU and ledger are updated, not duplicated.

Ledger settings

Safety stock = Z × σ(monthly sales) × √((lead time + review period) ÷ 30.4). Reorder point = daily sales × (lead time + check interval) + safety stock. Order up to = daily sales × (lead time + 2 × check interval) + safety stock. Months where stock was zero at both the start and end are left out, because sales were blocked, not low.
